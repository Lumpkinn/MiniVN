<h1>MiniVN</h1>
This is a small visual novel made fore hack club's terra!
The project in itself was mostly inspired by the season and all, since its basically spooky season already.

<h2>More info</h2>
The project was made with ren'py, and all of the backgrounds are free to use.
The general story outline is also included, if you want to see some of the planning behind the story.


<h3>Installation instructions</h3>
     How to run:
     Go to releases page, download the build appropriate for your machine, extract, and run.
